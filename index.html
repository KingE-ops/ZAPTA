The Current State

Identified Friction Points

An honest, analytical look at zaptatech.com's live web presence — and what it's silently costing you.

Current Site
Redesign Concept
Issue 01

Weak Visual Hierarchy

Core value propositions blend into the background. Visitors can't understand what makes ZAPTA special within the critical first 3 seconds — the window before they bounce.

Issue 02

Generic Color & Typography

The standard blue/white palette mirrors thousands of other agencies. There's nothing distinctive that signals "world-class AI software company" to a new visitor.

Issue 03

Static, No Interactions

No scroll animations, no micro-interactions. The site feels passive — inconsistent with the innovative, AI-forward identity ZAPTA projects in everything else it does.

Issue 04

Buried Social Proof

Terry Peterson's powerful video testimonial requires a manual click to play. This buried trust signal should be working automatically from the first scroll.

Issue 05

Empty Hero Real Estate

The right half of the hero section is wasted. A visually rich element — like a service orbit or animated visual — would massively improve engagement and dwell time.

Issue 06

Weak CTA Positioning

Calls-to-action are passive and poorly placed. High-intent visitors have to search for a way to engage — and most won't. Every extra click loses a potential client.

Business Reality
